TITLE: Sub2Unlock Safety Guide
TYPE: Link/monetization guide
STATUS: Safety-focused; task flow may change

PURPOSE
Sub2Unlock uses tasks or engagement requirements to unlock a destination link. NOVA prefers direct links and only discusses this service as a third-party obstacle.

SAFE APPROACH
1. Prefer asking the creator/sharer for a direct file link when possible.
2. If inspecting the page, use a normal browser and review what each task actually requests.
3. Never install software, browser extensions, APKs, or executables to unlock a Minecraft file.
4. Never provide passwords, payment information, recovery codes, or unnecessary personal data.
5. Never grant unexpected browser notification permissions.
6. If the page redirects to unrelated software or suspicious downloads, stop and leave the page.

NOVA POLICY
NOVA prefers direct downloads and does not require social tasks as a standard NOVA download method.

WHAT NOVA SHOULD NOT TEACH
- Do not teach methods to falsify completion of social tasks.
- Do not recommend bypass extensions as the default method.
- Do not claim a fixed number of tasks; the creator/service may change them.
