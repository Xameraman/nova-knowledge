Urlspay Link Unlock Guide

Urlspay is a URL shortener that requires multiple unlock rounds before revealing the final download link. NOVA never uses Urlspay – we only share direct links. This guide helps you safely navigate one if encountered.

---

⚡ Quick Start
Open in Chrome (not in‑app browser) → click Continue to Content → wait ~20s → tap Unlock Link → scroll down → tap Verify → tap Continue. Repeat 3–5 rounds until redirected to the file host. Never click ads or install anything.

---

🔓 UNLOCK STEPS

1. Open the Urlspay link in Chrome, Safari, or your device browser. Avoid in‑app browsers (Discord, YouTube, TikTok).
2. Click Continue to Content.
3. Wait ~20 seconds (countdown may appear). Stay on the page – don't switch tabs or let screen turn off.
4. Tap Unlock Link when active. Scroll all the way to the bottom.
5. Tap Verify at the bottom – wait for it to change to Continue, then tap it.
6. Repeat steps 3–5 for 3 to 5 rounds (sometimes up to 7).
7. After the final round, tap Unlock Link again, scroll down, and tap Continue or Get Link.
8. You'll be redirected to the actual file host (MediaFire, Google Drive, MEGA, etc.) – download from there.

❗ COMMON PROBLEMS

Problem Solution
Page reloads / progress resets Use Chrome or external browser – in‑app browsers break sessions.
Countdown restarts Stay on the page; don't switch tabs or let screen sleep.
Cannot find Verify button Scroll slowly to very bottom – it may be hidden under ads. Rotate to landscape.
Completed rounds but no link May need up to 7 rounds. If still fails, link is broken – ask for direct link.
"Virus detected" pop‑up Fake ad – close tab immediately. Do not click anything.
Pop‑up asks to install extension Never install – close tab. It's malware.

⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNINGS
· Never pay for upgrades, enter personal info, or install anything from Urlspay.
· Never click banner ads or pop‑ups – close unexpected tabs.
· Ad blockers may break the unlock – disable temporarily, then re‑enable.
· Use Chrome or Safari – in‑app browsers cause failures.
