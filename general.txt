NOVA is a community Discord + guide network for verified Minecraft Bedrock content: direct downloads, proper credit, 22 clean categories, zero ad links. Content is sourced primarily from CurseForge and MCPEDL, then re-hosted or linked through trusted mirrors.

⚡ Quick Start
Join Discord → get the Key Access role → browse a category channel → click a direct download link (CurseForge, MCPEDL, MediaFire, MEGA, Google Drive, Proton Drive). Installation help: guide hub linked below.

📥 Installing
Bedrock: download .mcpack/.mcaddon → open with Minecraft → activate in world/global resource settings.
Java: download .jar/.zip → place in .minecraft/resourcepacks (or mods/datapacks) → activate in-game.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Sharing on NOVA
· Packmakers (own creation): post in 📤 | Uploads here, or use /share-with-nova (name, file_link, category, credits, support_link, archive, photo) for a formal submission. Approved creators get the Verified Packmaker role.
· Sharers (someone else's content): use /nova-share (name, link, credits, description, image, category, optional support link). Bot posts an embed with 🚀 Download Now.
· Announcing approved submissions: use /nova-feature to add a permanent archive link plus up to three buttons: 🚀 Download Now, 🌌 Temp Mirror, 💝 Support the Creator.
· /nova-share and /nova-feature auto-announce — no extra steps.
