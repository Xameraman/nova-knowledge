📥 Installing
Bedrock: download .mcpack/.mcaddon → open with Minecraft → activate in world/global resource settings.
Java: download .jar/.zip → place in .minecraft/resourcepacks (or mods/datapacks) → activate in-game. Match mods to your exact Minecraft version (versions are now numbered like 26.1, 26.2).

🔎 Link policy
· NOVA's preferred sources are MCPEDL and CurseForge. Their content pages are verified first and are the usual source for pack submissions.
· Archive/mirror links are separate: Proton Drive, MediaFire and MEGA are supported archive options, used when a packmaker needs or requests an archive or provides their own valid archive link. They are not replacements for MCPEDL and CurseForge.
· NOVA prefers direct download links and does not intentionally use ad gates or link-unlock services. NOVA never uses Linkvertise, Urlspay, Sub2Unlock or Mirrored.to.
· If you meet an unexpected ad, link gate or redirect, report it with /nova-report (pack name + issue type) rather than assuming it is malicious.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Sharing on NOVA
· Packmakers (own creation): post in 📤 | Uploads here, or use /share-with-nova for a formal submission. Approved creators get the Verified Packmaker role.
· Sharers (an outside creator's content): use /nova-share with proper credit. /nova-feature features a pack from a NOVA packmaker.
· /nova-share and /nova-feature require the Experienced Nova Sharer role (the bot also allows a few staff roles). Administrators grant the Experienced Nova Sharer role after a user has successfully shared other people's creations correctly, without a single mistake. Having access to the server does not grant these commands.
· /share-with-nova is open to packmakers who have no special role — an unverified or unroled packmaker can submit their creation with it. The Verified Packmaker role comes after approval.
· /nova-share and /nova-feature announce automatically — nothing to publish or send afterwards. In an announcement channel the post is also published to servers following it.

📝 Command parameters (use these exact names)
/share-with-nova — required: name, file_link, secondary_tag, credits, description, support_link, archive, photo · optional: category, image_credit, video_link
/nova-share — required: name, link, credits, description, image, secondary_tag · optional: category, image_credit, support, video_link
/nova-feature — required: name, link, credits, description, image, secondary_tag, archive, support · optional: category, image_credit, video_link
Note: "support" (/nova-share, /nova-feature) and "support_link" (/share-with-nova) are different parameter names for different commands.
archive: required by /share-with-nova and /nova-feature, but a valid archive URL is not always available or allowed. If there is none, enter a placeholder such as "." or other appropriate text; enter a URL only if it is a valid archive link. In /share-with-nova the field hint is "YES / NO / or your own archive link": YES / NO is a permission request — YES means the packmaker wants NOVA to create an archive of their pack, NO means they don't — or they can enter their own valid archive link. The /nova-feature hint accepts "none".
