📥 Downloading & Installing Content
For Bedrock:
1. Download the .mcpack or .mcaddon file from a trusted direct link.
2. Open the file with Minecraft. The pack will import automatically.
3. Apply it in your world or global resources settings.

For Java:
1. Download the .jar or .zip file from a trusted direct link.
2. Place the file in your .minecraft/resourcepacks (or datapacks/mods) folder.
3. Activate it in the in‑game resource‑pack menu.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 How Sharing Works on NOVA

· Packmakers (your own creation): drop a direct link with a preview photo in 💌 request here, or use /share-with-nova for a formal submission (name, file_link, category, credits, support_link, archive, photo). Approved creators receive the Verified Packmaker role.
· NOVA Items Sharers (someone else’s content): use /nova-share with name, link, credits, description, image, category, and optional support link. The bot posts an embed with 🚀 Download Now.
· Experienced NOVA Sharers (announcing approved submissions): use /nova-feature to add a permanent archive link and up to three buttons: 🚀 Download Now, 🌌 Temp Mirror, 💝 Support the Creator.
· All /nova-share and /nova-feature actions are auto‑announced by the bot — no extra steps needed.

❗ Common Problems

Problem: I only see a few channels / can't see all 22 categories.
Solution: Go to the very top of the channel list in Discord. Below the server name, tap "Channels & Roles", then switch to the Roles tab. From there, you can either:
- Pick individual category roles (PvP, Furniture, etc.) – you'll get pinged when new content is added in those categories.
- Or grab the "Access all Without Pings" role to instantly see every channel without any notifications. The channels appear immediately after you select a role.

Problem: Broken link.
Solution: Use /nova-report.

Problem: Don’t know how to install.
Solution: Visit https://xameraman.github.io/guide-for-java-and-bedrock/

Problem: Found an ad link.
Solution: Report immediately with /nova-report.

Problem: Want to upload.
Solution: Decide if you are a packmaker or a sharer, then follow the matching method above.

Problem: NOVA Assistant not responding.
Solution: Wait a few seconds and try again, or ask in a support channel.

❓ FAQ

Is NOVA free?
Yes, everything on NOVA is and always will be free — no paywalls, no ad gates.

What file types are used?
Bedrock: .mcpack / .mcaddon. Java: .jar / .zip.

How do I get a broken link fixed?
Use the /nova-report command, and staff will handle it.

Can I share someone else’s creation?
Absolutely — use /nova-share and give proper credit. Never claim it as your own.

Why can’t I see all 22 category channels?
This is the #1 misunderstanding. You already have the Key Access role. To unlock more channels, go to the very top of the channel list, tap "Channels & Roles", then the "Roles" tab, and select the categories you want (or "Access all Without Pings" for everything with zero pings). The channels will appear instantly. You are not missing any downloads — the library is fully accessible once you pick the roles.
