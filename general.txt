WHAT IS NOVA

NOVA stands for Nexus Of Verified Assets — a community Discord server and guide network for Minecraft Bedrock Edition content, founded by Xameraman5022. NOVA provides verified, direct download links with proper creator credit, organized across 22 categories. No ad links or shorteners are ever allowed.

HOW TO JOIN AND USE NOVA

1. Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/Zc5pPEZYpe
2. Read the rules. English only. Moderation is handled by Wick bot.
3. Get the "Key Access" role to unlock most channels.
4. Browse the 22 categories: PvP, Utility, Furniture, Horror, Anime, Shader, Structure, Weapon, Bosses, Vehicle, Visual, Survival, Client, GUI & UI, Adventure, Minigame, Food, Environment, Addon, World, Animation, Skins.
5. Click the direct links in each channel — they go to trusted hosts like MediaFire, MEGA, Google Drive, Proton Drive. No ad links.
6. Need help downloading? Visit the guides hub: https://xameraman.github.io/all-nova-guides/
7. Creators: use direct links only. Bedrock: .mcpack/.mcaddon. Java: .jar/.zip. Always credit the original creator.

HOW SHARING WORKS IN NOVA (IMPORTANT — USE THIS TO DISTINGUISH COMMANDS)

There are two distinct groups of people who share content, and they use different channels/commands:

**PACKMAKERS (sharing your own creation):**
- You can simply drop your MCPEDL or direct creation link in the 💌 request here channel. A preview photo MUST be included; links without photos are ignored.
- For a formal submission, use /share-with-nova. Required fields: name, file_link, category, credits, support_link, archive (YES/NO/IDK/your own permanent link – filego.io is NEVER used), and a photo.
- Staff review submissions; approved packmakers get the Verified Packmaker role and are featured.

**NOVA ITEMS SHARERS (sharing someone else's content):**
- Use /nova-share. Required fields: name, direct download link, original creator credit, description, preview image, category, optional support link.
- The bot posts an embed with 🚀 Download Now and optionally 💝 Support the Creator buttons.

**EXPERIENCED NOVA SHARERS (announcing approved packmaker submissions):**
- Use /nova-feature. Adds a permanent archive link and up to three buttons: 🚀 Download Now, 🌌 Temp Mirror, 💝 Support the Creator.

ANNOUNCEMENT PROCESS:
After using /nova-share or /nova-feature, the announcer must publish both the text embed and a separate preview photo to the connected server (https://discord.gg/4HjDRTUc3c), text first, then photo.
