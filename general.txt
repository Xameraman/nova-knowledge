WHAT IS NOVA

NOVA stands for Nexus Of Verified Assets — a community Discord server and guide network for Minecraft Bedrock Edition content, founded by Xameraman5022. NOVA provides verified, direct download links with proper creator credit, organized across 22 categories. No ad links or shorteners are ever allowed.

HOW TO JOIN AND USE NOVA

1. Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/Zc5pPEZYpe
2. Read the rules. English only. Moderation is handled by Wick bot.
3. Get the "Key Access" role to unlock most channels.
4. Browse the 22 categories: PvP, Utility, Furniture, Horror, Anime, Shader, Structure, Weapon, Bosses, Vehicle, Visual, Survival, Client, GUI & UI, Adventure, Minigame, Food, Environment, Addon, World, Animation, Skins.
5. Click the direct links in each channel — they go to trusted hosts like MediaFire, MEGA, Google Drive, Proton Drive. No ad links.
6. Need help downloading? Visit the guides hub: https://xameraman.github.io/all-nova-guides/
7. Creators: use direct links only. Bedrock: .mcpack/.mcaddon. Java: .jar/.zip. Always credit the original creator.

HOW SHARING WORKS IN NOVA

NOVA has two ways to share content:

1. 💌 Request Here Channel (for packmakers):
   - Simply drop your MCPEDL or creation link in the channel.
   - If the link does not have a preview photo, your message will be ignored.
   - For detailed, structured submissions, use /share-with-nova.

2. /share-with-nova (for Packmakers):
   - Used by packmakers who want a formal submission.
   - Required fields: name, file_link, category, credits, support_link, archive preference (YES, NO, IDK, or a permanent link — filego.io is NEVER used), and a preview photo.
   - Staff review the submission. If approved, the packmaker gets the Verified Packmaker role and the pack is featured.

3. /nova-share (for NOVA Items Sharers):
   - Used by trusted members to share content from creators outside the server.
   - Required fields: name, link, credits, description, image, category, support (or "none").
   - The bot sends an embed with a 🚀 Download Now button and, if a support link is provided, a 💝 Support the Creator button.

4. /nova-feature (for Experienced NOVA Sharers):
   - Used to announce approved packmaker submissions.
   - Adds a permanent archive link (never filego.io) and can produce three buttons: 🚀 Download Now, 🌌 Temp Mirror, 💝 Support the Creator.

IMPORTANT: After announcing, the announcer must publish both the text embed and a separate preview photo to the connected server (https://discord.gg/4HjDRTUc3c), in that order.

All uploads must use direct links. No Linkvertise, Urlspay, Sub2Unlock wrappers. Every upload must credit the original creator. Images are mandatory.
