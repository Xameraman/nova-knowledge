TITLE: NOVA Creator & Packmaker Guide
TYPE: NOVA-specific policy

PURPOSE
This file tells NOVA how a user becomes a creator/packmaker and submits their
own Minecraft content (packs, addons, skins) through NOVA, as opposed to
sharing or finding someone else's content.

WHO THIS IS FOR
- Someone who made their own pack/addon/skin and wants it added to NOVA's
  library under their own name.
- Not for someone re-sharing another creator's work (see general.txt's
  "NOVA SHARING ROLES / FLOWS" section for that case instead -- that's a
  Sharer, not a Packmaker).

HOW TO SUBMIT YOUR OWN WORK
Two ways, both valid:

1. Quick way: post your MCPEDL, CurseForge, or MediaFire link directly in
   the 📤 | Uploads here channel. You must include a preview picture
   (a thumbnail or screenshot of the creation) -- submissions without one
   are ignored.
2. Formal way: use the /share-with-nova command for a more detailed
   submission. It takes: name, file_link, category, credits, support_link,
   archive, and photo.

Either way works -- the command just captures more structured detail up
front.

WHAT YOU NEED BEFORE SUBMITTING
- A preview picture (thumbnail or screenshot) -- required either way, no
  exceptions.
- Your MCPEDL / CurseForge / MediaFire link (quick way), or the full set of
  fields the command asks for (formal way): name, file_link, category,
  credits, support_link, archive link, photo.
- Confirmation the work is genuinely yours, or clear credit for anything
  it's based on.

BECOMING A RECOGNIZED CREATOR / PACKMAKER ROLE
Once a submission is approved, the creator receives the **Verified
Packmaker** role.

RULES FOR CREATORS
- Only submit work you made yourself, or clearly credit what it's based on.
- Do not claim someone else's pack as your own.
- Keep descriptions accurate; do not overstate compatibility or features.
- A preview picture is mandatory -- submissions without one are ignored.
- Broken or inappropriate NOVA links/submissions should be reported through
  /nova-report.

NOVA ASSISTANT BEHAVIOR FOR THIS TOPIC
- If asked "how do I upload/submit/become a packmaker", answer using the
  concrete steps above: 📤 | Uploads here (MCPEDL/CurseForge/MediaFire link
  + required preview picture) or /share-with-nova for the formal route.
- Do not invent a command, channel, or requirement not listed here.
- If this file is out of date (e.g. the channel gets renamed again), say
  you're not fully sure of the current exact steps and point the user to
  the NOVA Discord to ask staff directly, rather than guessing.
